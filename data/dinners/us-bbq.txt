@ Texas Smoked Brisket | BBQ | beef | smoker | 30 | 720 | 14 | hard | main
Whole packer brisket with a salt-and-pepper bark, smoked low until jiggly-tender.
- 1 whole packer brisket (12-14 lb)
- 1/4 cup kosher salt
- 1/4 cup coarse black pepper
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- Butcher paper
> Trim hard fat to 1/4 inch and remove any silver skin. Season all over with salt, pepper and garlic powder.
> Smoke at 250°F with oak or hickory, fat side up, until the bark is set and the internal temperature reaches 165°F, about 7-8 hours.
> Wrap tightly in butcher paper and continue smoking to 200-203°F, until a probe slides in like butter, 3-5 hours more.
> Rest wrapped in a cooler at least 1 hour (up to 4).
> Slice the flat against the grain into pencil-thick slices; cube the point for burnt ends.
