@ Beef Enchiladas with Chili Gravy | Tex-Mex | beef | stovetop,oven | 25 | 35 | 6 | medium | main
Old-school Texas enchiladas: corn tortillas, seasoned beef and cheddar under a brick-red chili gravy.
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, diced, divided
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 12 corn tortillas
- 3 cups cheddar, shredded
-- Chili gravy
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp flour
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1/2 tsp salt
> Heat oven to 400°F. Brown beef with half the onion, cumin and salt; drain.
> For the gravy, whisk oil and flour 2 minutes, add spices for 30 seconds, then whisk in broth and simmer until thick.
> Warm tortillas in a little oil or the microwave so they bend.
> Fill each with beef, cheese and raw onion; roll and place seam down in a 9x13 dish.
> Pour gravy over, top with remaining cheese and bake 15-20 minutes until bubbly.

Orange and spice-braised pork shoulder crisped under the broiler.

- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 onion, quartered
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 orange, juiced, peel reserved
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 cup water
- 2 bay leaves
- Corn tortillas, onion, cilantro and salsa verde
> Season pork with salt, cumin and oregano and put in a Dutch oven with onion, garlic, juices, orange peel, water and bay leaves.
