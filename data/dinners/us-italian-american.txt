Crispy breaded cutlets baked under marinara and melted mozzarella.
- 2 large chicken breasts, halved horizontally
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup Italian breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, divided
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 2 cups marinara sauce
- 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced

> Bake 12-15 minutes until bubbly. Top with basil and serve with spaghetti.
