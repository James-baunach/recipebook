@ Southern Fried Chicken | Southern | chicken | fry | 20 | 30 | 4 | medium | main
Buttermilk-brined chicken in a crisp, well-seasoned flour crust.
- 3 1/2 lb bone-in chicken pieces
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 1 tbsp hot sauce
- 2 1/2 tsp salt, divided
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1 quart peanut oil, for frying
> Whisk buttermilk, hot sauce and 1 tsp salt. Add chicken, cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
> Mix flour, remaining salt, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, pepper and cayenne in a shallow dish.
> Heat 1 1/2 inches of oil in a heavy skillet to 325°F.
> Lift chicken from buttermilk, dredge well in flour, pressing to coat. Rest on a rack 10 minutes.
> Fry in batches, skin side down first, turning occasionally, 12-15 minutes for white meat and 15-18 minutes for dark, until 165°F inside.
> Drain on a rack and season lightly with salt while hot.
! Keep the oil between 300-325°F for even browning without burning.

Cabbage leaves wrapped around turkey and rice, baked in sweet-tangy tomato sauce.
- 1 large head cabbage
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 cup cooked rice
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
