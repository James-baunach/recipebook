@ Cabbage and Noodles with Kielbasa | Midwest | pork | stovetop | 10 | 25 | 6 | easy | main
Haluski-style buttery fried cabbage, onions and egg noodles with sausage.
- 1 lb kielbasa, sliced
- 6 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 head cabbage, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 12 oz wide egg noodles, cooked
> Brown kielbasa in 1 tbsp butter; remove.
> Melt remaining butter and cook onion and cabbage 15-20 minutes until soft and golden.

@ Baked Pork Chops with Stuffing | Midwest | pork | oven | 10 | 45 | 6 | easy | main
Chops topped with stuffing and baked in mushroom gravy.
- 6 boneless pork chops
- 1 tsp seasoned salt
- 1 box (6 oz) stuffing mix
- 1 1/2 cups hot water
- 4 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup
- 1/3 cup milk
> Heat oven to 375°F. Season chops and place in a greased 9x13 dish.
> Mix stuffing with water and butter; mound on each chop.
> Whisk soup and milk and pour around the chops.
> Cover and bake 30 minutes, uncover and bake 15 minutes more.

@ Hamburger Gravy over Mashed Potatoes | Midwest | beef | stovetop | 10 | 20 | 4 | easy | main
Diner-style ground beef gravy spooned over creamy potatoes.
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp flour
- 2 cups milk
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups mashed potatoes
> Brown beef with onion; push aside and melt butter.
> Stir in flour and cook 1 minute.
> Add milk, broth and Worcestershire; simmer until thick. Season.
> Serve over mashed potatoes.

@ Cheesy Potato and Smoked Sausage Bake | Midwest | pork | oven | 15 | 50 | 6 | easy | main
Diced potatoes, smoked sausage and onions baked under melted cheddar.
- 2 lb potatoes, diced
- 1 lb smoked sausage, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups cheddar, shredded
> Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes, sausage, onion and pepper with oil and seasonings in a 9x13 pan.
> Roast 40 minutes, stirring halfway.
> Top with cheese and bake 5-10 minutes more until melted.
