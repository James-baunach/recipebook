@ Steak and Ale Pie | British | beef | oven,stovetop | 30 | 150 | 6 | hard | main
Braised beef and mushrooms in ale gravy under flaky pastry.
- 2 1/2 lb chuck, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 8 oz mushrooms, quartered
- 1 bottle (12 oz) brown ale
- 1 1/2 cups beef stock
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 1 sheet puff pastry
- 1 egg, beaten
> Season and flour beef; brown in oil. Add onion and mushrooms.
> Add ale, stock, Worcestershire and thyme; simmer covered 1 1/2-2 hours until tender. Cool.
> Heat oven to 400°F. Spoon filling into a pie dish, top with pastry and brush with egg.
> Bake 30 minutes until puffed and golden.

@ Lancashire Hotpot | British | lamb | oven | 25 | 150 | 6 | medium | main
Lamb and onions baked under overlapping layers of crisp sliced potatoes.
- 2 1/2 lb lamb neck or shoulder, cubed
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp flour
- 3 tbsp butter, divided
- 2 onions, sliced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 cups lamb or beef stock
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 2 lb potatoes, thinly sliced
> Season and flour lamb; brown in 1 tbsp butter.
> Layer lamb, onions and carrots in a casserole; add stock, Worcestershire and thyme.
> Overlap potato slices on top and brush with melted butter.
> Cover and bake at 325°F 2 hours; uncover and bake 30 minutes until the potatoes are crisp.

@ Chicken and Leek Pie | British | chicken | oven,stovetop | 25 | 45 | 6 | medium | main
Creamy chicken, leek and bacon pie with a puff pastry lid.
- 2 lb boneless chicken thighs, cubed
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4 slices bacon, chopped
- 3 leeks, sliced
- 3 tbsp flour
- 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp fresh thyme
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 sheet puff pastry
- 1 egg, beaten
> Brown chicken and bacon in butter. Add leeks and cook 6 minutes.
> Stir in flour, then stock, cream, mustard, thyme and salt; simmer until thick. Cool slightly.
> Pour into a pie dish, top with pastry and brush with egg.
> Bake at 400°F 30 minutes until golden.

Smoked haddock, salmon and shrimp in parsley sauce under mashed potatoes.

- 1 lb salmon
- 1/2 lb shrimp, peeled
- 2 1/2 cups milk
- 1 bay leaf
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 3 lb potatoes, mashed with butter
- 1/2 cup cheddar, shredded

@ Belgian Carbonnade Flamande | Belgian | beef | stovetop,oven | 20 | 150 | 6 | medium | main
Beef and onions braised in dark Belgian ale with mustard and brown sugar.
- 3 lb beef chuck, cubed
- 2 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 bottles (12 oz each) Belgian dubbel or brown ale
- 1 cup beef stock
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 4 sprigs thyme
- 2 bay leaves
> Season beef and brown in butter; remove.
> Cook onions 15 minutes until golden; stir in flour.
> Add beer, stock, sugar, vinegar, thyme and bay leaves; return beef.
> Spread mustard over the top, cover and braise at 325°F 2 1/2 hours. Serve with fries.
