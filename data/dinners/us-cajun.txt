@ Chicken and Andouille Gumbo | Cajun & Creole | chicken | stovetop | 30 | 120 | 8 | hard | soup
Dark-roux gumbo with chicken thighs, smoky andouille and the holy trinity.
- 3/4 cup vegetable oil
- 3/4 cup flour
- 1 large onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 3 ribs celery, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 8 cups chicken stock
- 2 lb boneless chicken thighs
- 1 lb andouille sausage, sliced
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp filé powder
- 4 green onions, sliced
- 6 cups cooked white rice
> Season chicken with Cajun seasoning and brown with the sausage in a heavy pot; remove.
> Add oil and flour and stir constantly over medium heat 30-45 minutes until the roux is the color of milk chocolate.
> Add onion, pepper and celery; cook 8 minutes. Add garlic.
> Slowly whisk in stock. Add bay leaves, thyme, cayenne, chicken and sausage.
> Simmer 1 1/2 hours, skimming fat. Shred chicken in the pot.
> Off heat stir in filé and green onions. Serve over rice.

Creamy eggplant, shrimp and trinity baked under a crunchy crumb top.
- 2 large eggplants, peeled and cubed
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and chopped
- 1 tsp Creole seasoning
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 cup seasoned breadcrumbs, divided
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
> Boil eggplant 10 minutes until soft; drain well and mash.
> Cook onion, pepper, celery and garlic in butter 8 minutes. Add shrimp and cook 2 minutes.

> Bake at 350°F 30 minutes until golden.
