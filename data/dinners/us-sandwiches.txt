@ Homemade Crunchwraps | Tex-Mex | beef | stovetop | 15 | 20 | 4 | medium | sandwich
Seasoned beef, queso and a crunchy tostada folded into a griddled tortilla.
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 packet (1 oz) taco seasoning
- 4 extra-large flour tortillas
- 4 tostada shells
- 1/2 cup nacho cheese sauce
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 cup Mexican-blend cheese, shredded
> Brown beef with taco seasoning and a splash of water.
> In the center of each tortilla layer beef and cheese sauce, then a tostada, sour cream, lettuce, tomato and cheese.
> Fold the tortilla edges toward the center in pleats to enclose.
> Cook seam side down in a dry skillet 2-3 minutes per side until golden.

@ Prime Rib French Dip Sliders | American | beef | oven | 10 | 15 | 12 | easy | sandwich
Leftover prime rib and horseradish cheddar on slider rolls with jus.
- 12 slider rolls
- 1 lb leftover prime rib or roast beef, thinly sliced
- 6 slices white cheddar
- 3 tbsp horseradish sauce
- 4 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 cups beef broth, warmed
> Heat oven to 350°F. Split the slab of rolls; spread bottoms with horseradish sauce.
> Layer beef and cheddar; replace tops and brush with garlic butter.
> Bake covered 10 minutes, uncovered 5 minutes.
> Serve with warm broth for dipping.

@ Barbecue Chicken Sandwiches | BBQ | chicken | slow-cooker | 10 | 240 | 8 | easy | sandwich
Shredded barbecue chicken piled on buns with pickles and slaw.
- 3 lb boneless chicken breasts or thighs
- 1 1/2 cups barbecue sauce
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 8 buns
- Pickles and coleslaw
> Put chicken in a slow cooker. Mix sauce, sugar, vinegar and spices and pour over.
> Cook on low 4 hours (or high 2 1/2 hours).
> Shred chicken in the sauce.
> Serve on buns with pickles and slaw.
