@ Classic Meatloaf | American | beef | oven | 20 | 65 | 6 | easy | main
Tender beef meatloaf with a sweet-tangy ketchup glaze.
- 2 lb ground beef (85% lean)
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 large eggs
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 cup plain breadcrumbs
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp dried thyme
-- Glaze
- 2/3 cup ketchup
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
> Heat oven to 350°F. Soak breadcrumbs in milk 5 minutes.
> Mix beef, onion, garlic, eggs, soaked crumbs, Worcestershire, salt, pepper and thyme with your hands until just combined.
> Shape into a 9x5-inch loaf on a foil-lined sheet pan.
> Stir glaze ingredients together and spread half over the loaf.
> Bake 45 minutes, brush with remaining glaze and bake 15-20 minutes more to 160°F. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.

- 3 1/2 lb boneless chuck roast
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, cut in wedges
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup red wine
- 2 cups beef broth
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 lb carrots, cut in 2-inch pieces
- 1 1/2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, halved
- 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water
> Heat oven to 325°F. Season roast with salt and pepper.

> Move meat and vegetables to a platter. Simmer the liquid, whisk in cornstarch slurry and cook until thick. Serve with the gravy.

- 2 1/2 lb chuck roast, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 6 cups beef broth
- 1 bag (24 oz) frozen thick egg noodles
- 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water
> Season beef and brown in oil. Add onion and cook 5 minutes.
> Add broth, cover and simmer 1 1/2-2 hours until the beef shreds.
> Shred beef in the pot. Add noodles and simmer 20 minutes until tender.
> Thicken with cornstarch slurry. Serve over mashed potatoes.

@ Pork Tenderloin Medallions with Mushroom Cream | American | pork | stovetop | 10 | 20 | 4 | easy | main
Quick-seared pork medallions in a garlicky mushroom cream sauce.
- 1 1/2 lb pork tenderloin, sliced 1 inch thick
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
> Flatten medallions slightly and season. Sear in oil 3 minutes per side; remove.
> Melt butter and brown mushrooms 6 minutes. Add garlic.
> Add broth, cream, mustard and thyme; simmer 3 minutes.
> Return pork and juices and simmer 2 minutes until 145°F.

@ Chicken Marbella-Style Roast | American | chicken | oven | 15 | 50 | 6 | easy | main
Chicken roasted with prunes, olives, capers and white wine — a dinner party classic.
- 3 1/2 lb bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks
- 1/2 cup pitted prunes
- 1/2 cup green olives
- 3 tbsp capers with juice
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp dried oregano
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 tsp salt
> Combine everything but the sugar and wine; marinate overnight.
> Heat oven to 350°F. Arrange chicken in one layer with marinade.
> Sprinkle with brown sugar and pour wine around.
> Bake 50-60 minutes, basting, until golden and 175°F.

@ Baked Ziti with Sausage | Italian-American | pork | oven | 20 | 40 | 8 | easy | main
Ziti baked in meaty tomato sauce with ricotta and melted mozzarella.
- 1 lb ziti
- 1 lb Italian sausage, casings removed
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
- 1 can (15 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 15 oz ricotta
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 3 cups mozzarella, shredded
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
> Heat oven to 375°F. Cook ziti 2 minutes short of al dente.
> Brown sausage with onion; add garlic, marinara, tomatoes and Italian seasoning and simmer 10 minutes.
> Mix ricotta, egg and Parmesan.
> Toss ziti with most of the sauce. Layer half in a 9x13 dish, dollop with ricotta, add 1 cup mozzarella, then the rest of the pasta and sauce.
> Top with remaining mozzarella and bake 25-30 minutes until bubbling.

@ Smothered Steak and Rice | American | beef | stovetop | 10 | 75 | 4 | easy | main
Sirloin strips braised with peppers and onions in brown gravy.
- 1 1/2 lb sirloin, sliced in strips
- 1 tsp seasoned salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp flour
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 cups cooked rice
> Season beef and toss with flour. Brown in oil; remove.
> Cook onion and pepper 5 minutes.
> Add broth and soy, return beef and simmer covered 1 hour until tender.
> Serve over rice.

@ Chicken Fricassee | American | chicken | stovetop | 15 | 45 | 4 | medium | main
Chicken braised in a light, creamy white wine and mushroom sauce.
- 3 lb bone-in chicken pieces
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 8 oz mushrooms, quartered
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 3 tbsp flour
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
> Season chicken and cook in butter skin side down until lightly golden. Remove.
> Cook onion, mushrooms and carrots 6 minutes. Stir in flour.
> Add wine, then broth, and return chicken. Cover and simmer 30 minutes.
> Stir in cream and lemon juice and simmer 5 minutes. Sprinkle with parsley.

@ Pepper Steak Skillet | American | beef | stovetop | 15 | 15 | 4 | easy | main
Strips of flank steak and bell peppers in a glossy soy-garlic sauce.
- 1 1/2 lb flank steak, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 3 tbsp soy sauce, divided
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1/2 cup beef broth
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tsp cornstarch
> Toss beef with 1 tbsp cornstarch and 1 tbsp soy.
> Sear beef in hot oil in batches until browned; remove.
> Stir-fry peppers and onion 3 minutes. Add garlic and ginger.
> Whisk broth, remaining soy, oyster sauce, sugar and 2 tsp cornstarch; add with the beef and simmer until glossy.
> Serve over rice.

@ Turkey Pot Pie with Biscuit Topping | American | turkey | oven,stovetop | 20 | 35 | 6 | easy | main
Leftover turkey and vegetables in gravy under a blanket of drop biscuits.
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 ribs celery, diced
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 cups turkey or chicken broth
- 1/2 cup milk
- 3 cups cooked turkey, diced
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 tsp poultry seasoning
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tube (16 oz) refrigerated biscuits
> Heat oven to 400°F. Cook onion, carrots and celery in butter in an ovenproof skillet 8 minutes.
> Stir in flour, then broth and milk; simmer until thick.
> Add turkey, peas, poultry seasoning and salt.
> Arrange biscuits on top and bake 18-22 minutes until golden.

@ Chicken Parmesan Meatballs | Italian-American | chicken | oven | 20 | 25 | 4 | easy | main
Ground chicken meatballs baked in marinara under bubbling mozzarella.
- 1 1/2 lb ground chicken
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 1 egg
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups marinara sauce
- 1 1/2 cups mozzarella, shredded
- 2 tbsp chopped basil
> Heat oven to 400°F. Mix chicken, panko, Parmesan, egg, garlic, Italian seasoning and salt.
> Roll into 16 meatballs and bake on a lined sheet 15 minutes.
> Spread marinara in a baking dish, add meatballs and top with mozzarella.
> Bake 10 minutes more until bubbly. Top with basil.

@ Sunday Roast Beef | American | beef | oven | 10 | 90 | 8 | medium | main
Garlic-rubbed top round roasted low for even, rosy slices.
- 4 lb top round or sirloin tip roast
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 6 cloves garlic, slivered
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 cup beef broth
> Salt roast and refrigerate overnight if you have time.
> Heat oven to 450°F. Cut small slits in the roast and insert garlic slivers. Rub with oil, pepper and rosemary.
> Roast 15 minutes, then lower to 275°F and roast until 125°F, about 1 1/4 hours.
> Rest 20 minutes. Deglaze the pan with broth for jus and slice thin.

@ Beef Pot Pie | American | beef | oven,stovetop | 25 | 120 | 6 | medium | main
Braised beef and vegetables in rich gravy topped with puff pastry.
- 2 lb chuck, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 cups beef broth
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
- 1 egg, beaten
> Season beef, toss in flour and brown in oil. Add onion, carrots, garlic and tomato paste.
> Add broth and Worcestershire, cover and simmer 1 1/2 hours until tender. Stir in peas.
> Heat oven to 400°F. Pour filling into a 2-quart baking dish.
> Lay pastry over the top, cut vents and brush with egg.
> Bake 25-30 minutes until puffed and golden.

@ Pan-Fried Pork Cutlets with Lemon | American | pork | stovetop | 15 | 10 | 4 | easy | main
Thin breaded pork cutlets fried golden and finished with lemon.
- 1 1/2 lb boneless pork loin, cut into 8 thin slices and pounded
- 1/2 cup flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 1/2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 lemon, cut in wedges
> Season cutlets. Dredge in flour, dip in egg and press into panko.
> Fry in hot oil 2 minutes per side until golden.
> Drain and serve with lemon wedges.
