@ Crab and Shrimp Seafood Casserole | Southern | shellfish | oven | 20 | 30 | 6 | easy | main
Shrimp and crab baked in a sherry cream sauce with a cracker top.
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled
- 8 oz lump crabmeat
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
- 3 tbsp flour
- 1 1/2 cups half-and-half
- 2 tbsp dry sherry
- 1 tsp Old Bay seasoning
- 1 cup cooked rice
- 1 cup crushed crackers
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
> Heat oven to 350°F. Cook onion and pepper in butter 5 minutes. Stir in flour, then half-and-half; simmer until thick.
> Add sherry, Old Bay, shrimp, crab and rice.
> Pour into a baking dish and top with crackers tossed in melted butter.
> Bake 25-30 minutes until bubbling.
