@ Chicken Lazone | Cajun & Creole | chicken | stovetop | 10 | 15 | 4 | easy | main
Spice-dusted chicken strips in a rich butter-cream sauce from Louisiana.
- 1 1/2 lb chicken tenders
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
> Toss chicken with the spices and salt.
> Cook in 2 tbsp butter 3 minutes per side. Remove.
> Add cream and simmer 3 minutes, scraping the pan. Swirl in remaining butter.
> Return chicken and coat. Serve over pasta or rice with parsley.

@ Chicken with Artichokes and Lemon | American | chicken | stovetop | 10 | 25 | 4 | easy | main
Seared chicken simmered with artichoke hearts, capers and lemon.
- 4 boneless chicken breasts, halved horizontally
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (14 oz) artichoke hearts, quartered
- 2 tbsp capers
- 3/4 cup chicken broth
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 tbsp butter
> Season and flour chicken; brown in oil 3 minutes per side. Remove.
> Add garlic and artichokes and brown 3 minutes.
> Add capers, broth and lemon juice; simmer 3 minutes. Swirl in butter.
> Return chicken and simmer until cooked through.
