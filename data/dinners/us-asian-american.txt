Roasted tenderloin glazed with hoisin, honey and five-spice.
- 2 pork tenderloins
- 1/3 cup hoisin sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp five-spice powder
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
> Mix hoisin, honey, soy, five-spice and garlic. Marinate pork 1-8 hours.

Air-fried chicken bites in a sweet-hot honey sriracha glaze.
- 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, cubed
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, minced
> Toss chicken in egg, then cornstarch and salt. Spray with oil.

> Simmer honey, sriracha, soy, vinegar and garlic 2 minutes.
> Toss chicken in glaze.

- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp oil

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
